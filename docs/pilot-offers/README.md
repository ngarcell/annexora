# Pilot Offers (Top 5 Markets)

This folder contains one-page, fixed-scope paid pilot offers for the top five markets:

- United Kingdom (GBP)
- United States (USD)
- Germany (EUR)
- France (EUR)
- Netherlands (EUR)

## What these offers are

These are **audit-readiness** offers, not legal or regulatory advice. They are designed to:

- Explain the 4-week, 2-system pilot clearly
- Keep scope fixed and outcomes tangible
- Make procurement and legal review easier

## What these offers are not

We do **not**:

- Interpret the law on the customer’s behalf
- Issue conformity assessments
- Act as a notified body
- Provide legal opinions or certifications

## Pricing policy

- Base price: **£15,000 (UK)**
- Other currencies are converted from GBP using a spot rate on the day of generation
- Prices are rounded to the nearest **1,000** units
- VAT/sales tax is excluded unless explicitly stated

See `pricing-table.md` for the FX rates and conversion calculations.
