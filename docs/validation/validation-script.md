# 5-Call Validation Script (Master + Country Addendums)

## Goal

Validate that Annex III deployers will pay for a fixed-scope, 4-week audit-readiness pilot, and confirm scope, objections, and decision process.

## Target persona

- Compliance / Risk / Legal leader, or product owner with compliance budget
- Company deploying AI in EU markets (or planning to)

---

## Call 1: Problem discovery

**Objective:** Confirm compliance pressure, systems scope, and urgency.

**Questions**

- Which AI systems are in scope for EU AI Act review in the next 12 months?
- Who owns documentation, evidence, and audit readiness today?
- What happens if you cannot show evidence quickly?
- What is your internal deadline for audit readiness?

**Success signal**

- Multiple high-risk systems
- Clear urgency tied to audit or regulatory milestone

---

## Call 2: Current process + cost of chaos

**Objective:** Quantify internal friction and cost of delays.

**Questions**

- Where does evidence live today (tools, teams, owners)?
- What takes the most time when preparing audit documentation?
- How often are decisions delayed because evidence is incomplete?
- What would faster evidence packaging be worth to you?

**Success signal**

- Evidence is scattered across teams and tools
- Delays or rework are already occurring

---

## Call 3: Solution reaction

**Objective:** Test the pilot deliverables and value proposition.

**Pitch (short)**

- 4-week pilot, 2 high-risk systems
- Inventory + classification + obligation mapping + audit-ready pack
- Outcome: traceability matrix and evidence bundle ready for review

**Questions**

- Which deliverable is most valuable?
- What is missing for this to be compelling?
- Would this help your internal legal/compliance review process?

**Success signal**

- Clear “yes” on deliverables and outcome

---

## Call 4: Pricing validation

**Objective:** Confirm price acceptance and procurement flow.

**Questions**

- Would a fixed-scope pilot at [LOCAL PRICE] be approved?
- Who signs off on this level of spend?
- What procurement/security steps are required?

**Success signal**

- Pricing within approval threshold or clear steps to approval

---

## Call 5: Close / next steps

**Objective:** Confirm decision process and schedule next step.

**Questions**

- What is the fastest path to a readiness review call?
- Who should be included (legal, risk, ML, procurement)?
- What date would you like the pilot to start?

**Success signal**

- Clear decision owner + timeline + next meeting scheduled

---

# Country Addendums

Use the following inserts during Call 4 and in follow-up notes.

## UK

- **Local price:** GBP 15,000
- **Procurement nuance:** Expect a vendor onboarding questionnaire and security review, but fixed scope helps speed approval.
- **Legal framing:** We provide audit-readiness evidence workflows; legal applicability remains with customer counsel.
- **Common objection:** “We will wait until guidance is finalized.”
  - Response: “The pilot builds evidence infrastructure that remains useful regardless of guidance changes.”

## US

- **Local price:** USD 21,000
- **Procurement nuance:** Expect security and privacy reviews; emphasize EU-market scope and fixed deliverables.
- **Legal framing:** EU AI Act applicability depends on EU deployment or EU market exposure; confirm with counsel.
- **Common objection:** “We are US-based; does this apply?”
  - Response: “If systems are deployed in the EU or serve EU residents, readiness is required. The pilot validates scope quickly.”

## Germany

- **Local price:** EUR 17,000
- **Procurement nuance:** Expect legal and data protection review; fixed scope reduces procurement friction.
- **Legal framing:** We provide audit-ready evidence infrastructure, not legal opinions.
- **Common objection:** “We already have documentation.”
  - Response: “The pilot maps evidence to obligations and creates a traceability matrix for audit defense.”

## France

- **Local price:** EUR 17,000
- **Procurement nuance:** Expect legal/compliance review and standard vendor onboarding.
- **Legal framing:** We provide readiness workflows aligned to Annex III obligations; no conformity assessments.
- **Common objection:** “We prefer to wait for a notified body.”
  - Response: “Notified bodies move faster when evidence is organized. The pilot reduces time and rework.”

## Netherlands

- **Local price:** EUR 17,000
- **Procurement nuance:** Expect security review and DPA checks; fixed scope simplifies approval.
- **Legal framing:** We provide audit-readiness evidence workflows, not legal advice.
- **Common objection:** “We need budget approval.”
  - Response: “We can scope two systems now and expand later without rework.”
